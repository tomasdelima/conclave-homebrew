# Individual monster playtest records

These are unplaytested design targets. Raw DPR assumes all listed ordinary attacks hit and one two-use Legendary Attack where available. Conditions, Advantage, Resistance, and special-action alternatives require encounter testing. The midpoint assumes feeding on half of rounds. No live 5etools import or tabletop balance check has been completed.

## Tidewrack Hand

CR 1/8; coast; fear; finisher. HP 7 (2d4 + 2). Ankle Grip: +4 to hit, 2.5 average damage (1d4). Save DC 11.

Raw DPR without feeding: 2.5; with feeding: 4; half feeding uptime: 3.25. Three-round totals: 7.5, 12, and 9.75 respectively.

Counter: It can impose Frightened or deal feeding damage only while both it and its target are in water no deeper than 2 feet. Dry ground or deeper water prevents both abilities.

Intended first playtest: four rested characters of levels 1–2. Fractional CR creatures are nuisance encounters, not solo bosses. CR 21+ assumes a prepared party and terrain with escape routes.
