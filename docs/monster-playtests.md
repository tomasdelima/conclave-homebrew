# Individual monster playtest records

These are unplaytested design targets. Raw damage per round assumes every listed ordinary attack
hits, plus one two-use Legendary Attack where the creature has Legendary Actions. It excludes
recharge actions, lair actions, saving throws, Resistance, Advantage, and critical hits. The
midpoint assumes the monster's emotion trigger is live half the rounds — that is, that the party
finds and holds its counter for about half the fight.

Clan traits are terrain- and formation-dependent, so these figures carry an assumption about
where the fight happens: coast and confluence numbers assume water, mines numbers assume no
uncovered open flame within 30 feet, and walled-city numbers assume targets standing within 5
feet of an ally. Moving the fight moves the numbers, which is the point of a clan trait.

Raw damage per round rises monotonically with Challenge Rating across the collection, as do save
DC, attack bonus, AC, and Hit Points, with deliberate dips where a role calls for them: a
conditional defender trades damage for Resistance, and an attrition threat trades it for healing.
Roles are listed per creature below.

No live toolset import and no tabletop balance check has been completed.

## Tidewrack Hand

CR 1/8; Coast and sea (Tidebound); fear; finisher.
AC 12; HP 7 (2d4 + 2); save DC 11.
Ankle Grip: +4 to hit, 2.5 average (1d4), 1 per Multiattack.

Raw damage per round with the trigger off: 2.5; with it on: 4; half-uptime midpoint: 3.25. Three-round totals: 7.5, 12, 9.75.

Counter: Stand on dry rock: on dry ground the hand's Speed is halved, it has Disadvantage on attack rolls, and it can't grip an ankle it can't reach through water.

Intended first playtest: four rested characters of levels 1–2.
