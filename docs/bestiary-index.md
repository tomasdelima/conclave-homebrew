## Individual playtest records

The machine-readable records in `monster-notes.json` include HP and attack formulas, save DCs, three-round damage with feeding on and off, the half-uptime planning estimate, party assumptions, counters, and fused organ thresholds. Raw damage is before accuracy and saves; Advantage, regeneration, conditional Resistance, and control need separate evaluation.

## Condition interactions

Fear is not the only source of Blinded or Deafened. Grief can suppress senses, longing can bind or move prey, envy can restrain a rival, and pride can punish a failed save. Effects retain the durations and escape routes printed in each stat block. Ordinary condition immunities still work. No creature receives an unlisted extra turn because a feeding trigger appears.

## Verification status

Local structural, arithmetic, asset, and coverage checks are automated. No live 5etools import or tabletop balance test has been completed.
